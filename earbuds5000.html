1. CMF by Nothing Buds Pro 2

CMF by Nothing Buds Pro 2

₹3,699

The CMF by Nothing Buds Pro 2 is a premium pair of true wireless earbuds designed for users who want high-quality sound, strong noise cancellation, and modern smart features. These earbuds feature a dual-driver setup with an 11mm bass driver and a 6mm micro-planar tweeter for rich and balanced audio. With hybrid Active Noise Cancellation up to 50dB, six HD microphones for clear calls, and Bluetooth 5.3 connectivity with LDAC support, the earbuds deliver an immersive listening experience.
