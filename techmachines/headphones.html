Conclusion: Pick Your Perfect Work Headphones

Best Overall: Soundcore Life Q35 (₹2,999) - With premium sound quality, excellent active noise cancellation, and 50-hour battery life, this is the best headphone for work and study sessions. You get professional-grade features at a reasonable price.

Best Budget Option: boAt Rockerz 450 (₹1,699) - If you're on a tight budget, these offer incredible 40-hour battery life and comfort at the lowest price. Perfect for students.

Best Sound Quality: Skullcandy Crusher EVO (₹2,199) - For music lovers and those who want excellent sound with customizable bass, this is the perfect choice at a great price point.

Best Battery Life: Realme Dizo GH1 Pro (₹2,499) - With a mind-blowing 80-hour battery life, this headphone is perfect if you travel frequently and don't want to charge often.

All these headphones are excellent choices for work and study. Your decision should depend on whether you need active noise cancellation, sound quality, battery life, or budget constraints. Pick the one that aligns with your priorities, and enjoy hours of comfortable listening!
