Conclusion: Choose Your Perfect Smartwatch

Best Overall: Amazfit Bip 3 Pro (₹1,299) - If you want the complete package with GPS, great battery life, and excellent features, the Amazfit Bip 3 Pro is our top recommendation. It's the best all-rounder for the price.

Best Budget Option: Redmi Watch 2 (₹999) - For the lowest price with a beautiful AMOLED display and 12-day battery life, the Redmi Watch 2 is unbeatable. Perfect if you're tight on budget.

Best Battery Life: Fire-Boltt Dazzle 2 (₹1,099) - With 14 days of battery life and an affordable price, this is perfect if you don't want to charge your watch constantly.

Best for Calling: Noise ColorFit Ultra 2 (₹1,199) - If you want to take calls directly from your wrist, this watch offers that feature at a reasonable price.

All these smartwatches are excellent choices under ₹1500. Consider your priorities: budget, battery life, GPS, or specific features like calling. Whatever you choose, you'll get a great smartwatch that'll keep you connected and healthy!
